AI digest for 2026-10-14 — 4 items

> **Today's big item:** Google made Gemini 4 Argon, its top model, available to all developers through the Gemini API and AI Studio. It reads and writes up to 1M tokens in total, and Google says it resists indirect prompt injection (hidden instructions in content the model reads) better than other models. It costs $2 in / $10 out per million tokens until November 30, then doubles.

## New model launches

- **Google opens Gemini 4 Argon to all developers**
  The model that launched for a small group on September 30 is now on the paid Gemini API tier with a 1M-token input-plus-output limit, so you can try it in your own agents and RAG (feeding docs to an LLM) apps.
  https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-argon/

- **Liquid AI releases LFM2.5-24B-A2B for on-device agents**
  An open-weights MoE (only some parts run per word) model with 24B parameters but only about 2B active, built for tool calling on a laptop, with GGUF and MLX builds and support in llama.cpp and vLLM.
  https://huggingface.co/LiquidAI/LFM2.5-24B-A2B

## New techniques / papers

- **Agent-Reflex trains agents to recover from their own mistakes**
  A three-step training recipe teaches a Qwen3-8B agent to notice bad tool results, fix its course and accept user corrections, and it beat other methods on five long tool-use benchmarks.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17953

- **SwitchMem picks text or screenshot memory for each step of a GUI agent**
  Agents that click through apps do better when they choose, step by step, whether to recall past experience as text or as images, instead of always using one format.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17928
