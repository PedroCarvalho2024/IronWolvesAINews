## New techniques / papers

- **SpecAgent lets a small model draft agent steps for a big one**
  A cheap model drafts tool-calling steps and a strong model checks them in one pass, matching the strong model on τ²-Bench within one point while cutting wall-clock time roughly in half.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21463
