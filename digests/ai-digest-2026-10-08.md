> **Today's big item:** Anthropic released Claude Haiku 5.5, its new small, fast model, as `claude-haiku-5-5` on the API, Bedrock and Vertex AI. It costs $0.10 per million input tokens for prompts up to 100k tokens, about 90% less than Haiku 4.5, and scores far higher on agent and coding benchmarks. It is also the first Haiku with an adjustable effort setting, so it is a strong pick for cheap subagents and high-volume jobs.

## New model launches

  https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4/

## New techniques / papers

- **Not every agent call needs a frontier model**
  Testing 9 models across five LLM call sites in a home-automation agent, the best local model matched hosted models at four of five sites, scoring 91.8% versus 95.4% at zero per-call cost.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09021

## Ontologies & knowledge graphs for AI

- **BEACON-SP pairs an ontology with GraphRAG for clinical questions**
  It links patient knowledge graphs to an ontology (a formal map of concepts and how they relate) to guide retrieval, and beat plain vector RAG on completeness and grounding across 1,500 clinical queries.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09026

## RAG / vector DB tooling

- **Google releases EmbeddingGemma 2, a small multimodal embedding model**
  A 740M-parameter Apache 2.0 model that turns text, code, images, audio and video into vectors (number lists used for search) in one shared space, and runs on a phone, handy for local or privacy-sensitive RAG (feeding docs to an LLM).
  https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/embeddinggemma-2/

- **Stored multi-vector document indexes can leak the original pages**
  Researchers rebuilt 47% of the words and 45% of the sensitive tokens from raw visual document embeddings, so treat your vector store as sensitive data, not as a safe anonymised copy.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09920

## AI security & compliance

- **Phishing emails now hide prompt injection aimed at AI inbox assistants**
  Barracuda found a live campaign with hidden instructions meant to make summarising assistants call the email urgent or legitimate, and it recommends stripping hidden content and keeping human approval for payments.
  https://blog.barracuda.com/2026/10/07/email-attacks-target-both-humans-ai-assistants
